Rumi and the Golden Egg – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Rumi and the Golden Egg ("the Game", package com.rumigoldenegg.game) handles information. The Game is suitable for children and families, and we treat every player as a child.

Information the Game itself stores

The Game has no accounts, no chat and no way to share content. It does not ask for a name, email, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. Game progress (finished levels, stars, golden feathers, journal clues and sound settings) is saved only on the device and is deleted when the Game is uninstalled.

Advertising

The Game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and its ad network partners. Because the Game is for children:

  • every ad request is marked as child-directed (COPPA), so ads are not personalized and no interest-based or behavioral advertising is used;
  • the Game does not access the device's advertising ID (the permission is removed from the app);
  • only ad networks that have self-certified compliance with Google Play's Families policy are allowed.

To deliver ads, limit how often they appear and prevent fraud, the ad providers may automatically process limited technical information: IP address (used for approximate country-level location), the app set ID, device and operating-system information (model, OS version, language, screen size, network type) and which ads were shown or tapped. This is processed under the providers' own policies, including the Unity / ironSource privacy policy: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

When ads appear

A short ad may appear between levels (not more than once every few levels, never during play). When Rumi runs out of hearts, players can choose to watch a video ad to keep going from the same spot, or return to the last checkpoint for free. Watching ads is never required to finish the Game.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The Game contains no in-app purchases, no social features and no links to other apps except those inside ads, which are shown by the ad providers under the child-directed rules above. If you are a parent or guardian and believe information about your child was collected, contact us and we will help delete it.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. Apart from the advertising providers described above, no data is shared with third parties.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
